Seminar discussion

What would you recommend?

Choose a question after forming your own answer.

Why did we choose three groups?
Example answer

The analyst set k to 3. At k = 2 the silhouette is higher (0.673), but 279 authorities fall into one group and 17 into the other. Three groups separate different patterns of provision and give Nottingham a more specific benchmark.

Would giving rapid-charger share more weight change the shortlist?
Example answer

Doubling its weight in the squared distance puts Bedford first, Broxbourne second and Teignbridge third. All three remain on the list. Removing standardisation changes two of the three peers.

Which evidence would you collect before choosing a site?
Example answer

I would ask for utilisation and queueing by hour, local EV ownership, and the share of households without off-street parking. Then I would check the cost of a grid connection and nearby competitors’ plans.

How would you check the code suggested by Copilot?
Example answer

I would check that both input columns have mean zero and standard deviation one after scaling, then inspect the cluster sizes. For the peer ranking I would exclude Nottingham, filter to its cluster, and sort by distance in that same scaled space.

Would you invest £2 million in a Teignbridge charging hub?
Example answer

I would first request monthly utilisation at nearby rapid chargers. Strong demand through winter would support further site assessment. A summer-only peak could favour a smaller first phase. A similar supply profile alone cannot establish demand at the proposed site.
